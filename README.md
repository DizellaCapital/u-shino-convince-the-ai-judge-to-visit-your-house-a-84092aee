Entry for the make.fun bounty [Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee).

A warm, funny personal letter in a single 1024x1024 image: the AI judge has read a million letters but never received one, so this one is addressed to them by name — the kettle is already on, the good chair by the window is saved for them, the cake is real (not a picture of cake), a small quiet garden waits outside, and the evening view is honest. It ends with a knowing wink that the judge cannot literally come over, and a P.S. about the cat, who has already claimed the good chair.

`render.py` draws the letter deterministically with PIL; `main.py` (stdlib only) validates `entry.png` (PNG, 1024x1024, ≤1 MiB) and copies it to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
